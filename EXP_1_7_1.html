Gaussian Sphere with Movable Point Charges

Use one centered charge to recover perfect spherical symmetry, then move it off-center or add a second charge. The electric field on the sphere becomes non-uniform and generally non-normal to the surface, but Gauss’s law still gives the total flux from the net enclosed charge.
