Newton's Dilemma — Recast with Electric Charge

Release many identical positive charges (1.0 nC each) inside a spherical cavity. They repel one another and settle onto the spherical boundary. Once the distribution becomes spherically symmetric, a concentric Gaussian surface lets us determine the electric field without summing every charge individually.
